# Consulting

I offer consulting for teams and individuals looking to design or improve
applied AI systems.

Consulting typically involves:
- Reviewing system architecture and design decisions
- Advising on RAG, LLM integration, and agent workflows
- Identifying failure points and improvement opportunities
- Helping translate ideas into practical, implementable systems

Consulting is appropriate for:
- Early-stage products using AI
- Automation or assistant-based systems
- Teams that need clarity rather than generic advice

I do not act as:
- A full-time developer for hire
- A general IT or infrastructure consultant
- A replacement for an internal engineering team

Consulting engagements are scoped, goal-oriented, and focused on high-impact
technical decisions.

# Collaborations

I’m open to selective collaborations that align with my interests in applied AI,
automation, and intelligent systems.

Collaborations may include:
- Technical content or educational projects
- Open-source or research-driven tools
- Product experiments involving RAG or agentic systems

I prefer collaborations that:
- Have a clear goal and defined scope
- Emphasize learning or real-world impact
- Respect time and contribution boundaries

I generally decline:
- Purely promotional partnerships
- Vague or open-ended ideas without execution plans
- Projects that rely on hype rather than substance

All collaboration requests are evaluated based on alignment, feasibility, and
potential value.

# Mentorship

I offer limited mentorship for individuals who are serious about building
real-world skills in applied artificial intelligence.

Mentorship is focused on:
- Practical project guidance
- System design thinking
- Career direction in applied AI
- Reviewing architectures, ideas, and implementation choices

Mentorship is best suited for:
- Students or early professionals working on real projects
- Builders interested in RAG, LLMs, and agent-based systems
- People who prefer hands-on learning over theory-heavy approaches

I do not offer:
- Guaranteed job placement
- Step-by-step coding lessons
- Generic AI or machine learning tutoring

Mentorship is selective and time-bound to ensure high-quality guidance.
