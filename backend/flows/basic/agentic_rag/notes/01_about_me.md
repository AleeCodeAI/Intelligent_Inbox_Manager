# Bio

I am an Applied AI Engineer focused on building practical, production-ready
AI systems rather than academic or purely theoretical models.

My work primarily revolves around Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval
Augmented Generation (RAG), and agent-based systems that solve real-world
problems such as automation, decision support, and intelligent workflows.

I specialize in designing end-to-end AI pipelines — from data ingestion and
preprocessing to retrieval, reasoning, and action execution. My approach
emphasizes clarity, reliability, and scalability over hype.

I actively work on:
- AI-powered automation systems
- Intelligent email and workflow assistants
- RAG-based knowledge systems
- Agentic AI architectures for decision-making

I prefer building tools that reduce cognitive load, save time, and increase
human productivity rather than replacing human judgment entirely.

Most of my work is self-driven, project-based, and focused on long-term
skill mastery in applied artificial intelligence.

# Journey

My journey into applied artificial intelligence started with a strong focus on
foundations rather than shortcuts.

I began by learning programming and core computer science concepts, then moved
into machine learning and modern AI systems with a practical mindset. Instead
of following purely academic paths, I focused on building real projects that
solve clear problems.

Over time, my focus shifted toward:
- Large Language Models and their real-world applications
- Retrieval Augmented Generation for reliable AI responses
- Agent-based systems that can reason, decide, and act
- Automation tools that integrate AI with existing workflows

I learned primarily through hands-on experimentation, reverse engineering
systems, and building end-to-end applications rather than isolated demos.

My long-term goal is to design intelligent systems that are useful, dependable,
and aligned with how humans actually work.

# Values

I value practicality over theory when it comes to applied artificial intelligence.
While strong fundamentals matter, real-world usefulness is the ultimate measure
of a system’s success.

I believe AI systems should:
- Be understandable and maintainable
- Produce reliable and grounded outputs
- Assist human decision-making rather than replace it
- Respect user intent and context

I prioritize:
- Clean system design
- Clear boundaries between automation and human control
- Ethical use of AI without unnecessary complexity or hype

I am intentional about the projects I take on and prefer working on problems
where AI provides genuine leverage instead of novelty.
